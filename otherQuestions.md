# Research Branches and Other Questions

The project now has a foundation-first [master outline](outline.md). Several subjects previously kept here have moved into that core because the argument cannot be stated cleanly without them:

- anthropic reasoning and self-location;
- observer-history measures and observer census problems;
- simulation as an implementation relationship;
- Many-Worlds as a measure stress test;
- recursive observer reproduction;
- variable host and child laws;
- Smolin as an analogue for reproductive selection;
- quantum history spaces as a possible architecture for later purpose hypotheses.

This file retains research branches that can deepen those sections but should not silently become premises. Load-bearing unresolved questions appear first.

## Priority: Measure and Reproduction

### What regularizes nonconvergent recursive observer measure?

Job: Replace the placeholder word `normalization` with a physically and epistemically explicit rule.

The finite stationary matrix model now supplies a clean negative result: when an accessible reproductive mode has spectral radius at least one, the unnormalized sum over generations does not converge. Normalized type proportions can sometimes settle, but that answers a different question and does not define a sample over all observer histories.

The remaining issue is not whether one can divide by a growing total. It is which population the model says an observer is sampled from:

- Does the cutoff operate by causal depth, host time, child time, computation, energy, or another resource?
- Does reproduction saturate as descendants compete for shared resources?
- Are lineages independent enough for a branching model, or does common infrastructure couple their fates?
- Which conclusions remain stable across defensible cutoff or saturation rules?

Destination: Produce sensitivity results across explicit regularizations. Do not adopt a cutoff merely because it yields a finite answer.

Research anchors:

- [Matrix Analysis and Applied Linear Algebra](https://doi.org/10.1137/1.9781611977448) by Carl D. Meyer (2023), for the finite matrix-series result.
- [Galton-Watson and Branching Process Representations of the Normalized Perron-Frobenius Eigenvector](https://doi.org/10.1051/ps/2019007) by Cerf and Dalmau (2019), for the distinction between growth and normalized type composition under primitive branching assumptions.
- [An Approximation of Populations on a Habitat with Large Carrying Capacity](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00285-024-02069-w) by Bauman, Chigansky, and Klebaner (2024), for a density-dependent alternative to unconstrained branching.

## Physical Parent Universes

### Can universes have parents?

Job: Investigate models in which black holes or other transitions produce new expanding regions of spacetime.

A singularity in general relativity is not evidence of a doorway. It marks a failure of the classical description. Relevant questions include:

- Which quantum-gravity or limiting-curvature models replace a black-hole singularity with a bounce or expanding region?
- Would a child remain connected through a horizon or pinch off causally?
- Could anything cross the boundary?
- Could either side observe the other?

Destination: Physical ancestry is one possible relationship between realities. It is not automatically computation, simulation, intention, or design.

Research anchors:

- [The Universe as a Black Hole](https://doi.org/10.1038/240298a0) — R. K. Pathria (1972)
- [Through a black hole into a new universe?](https://doi.org/10.1016/0370-2693(89)91114-3) — Frolov, Markov, and Mukhanov (1989)
- [Black holes as possible sources of closed and semiclosed worlds](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.41.383) — Frolov, Markov, and Mukhanov (1990)

### Was the Big Bang a white hole?

Job: Test the analogy rather than treating it as an identity.

A white hole is the time reverse of a black hole in particular general-relativistic solutions. A standard expanding cosmology is not therefore an ordinary white hole. Ask which additional bounce, brane, holographic, or extra-dimensional assumptions make the comparison meaningful and whether any version differs observationally from ordinary inflationary cosmology.

Destination: One boundary might admit radically different descriptions from opposite causal sides, but the Big Bang/white-hole mapping is a family of speculative models.

Research anchor:

- [Out of the White Hole: A Holographic Origin for the Big Bang](https://arxiv.org/abs/1309.1487) — Pourhasan, Afshordi, and Mann (2014)

### What does a child universe inherit?

Job: Separate effective child laws from deeper transition rules.

Could constants, dimensionality, symmetries, or low-energy laws vary across a birth event? If observable laws vary, what meta-law governs the transition? What would inheritance and mutation mean physically?

This is where Smolin's cosmological natural selection belongs as a physical analogue. Its inheritance and small-mutation assumptions are claims within that hypothesis, not general consequences of universe reproduction.

Destination: Parent and child physics need not match. This supports the core distinction between `theta_H` and `theta_C` without establishing any particular transition mechanism.

Research anchors:

- [The fate of black hole singularities and the parameters of the standard models of particle physics and cosmology](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9404011) — Lee Smolin (1994)
- [The status of cosmological natural selection](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0612185) — Lee Smolin (2006)

## Creator Pathways

### Can we reason about an upstream reality?

Job: Make the extrapolation problem explicit.

If a creator evolved under physics like ours, our knowledge of energy, computation, evolution, and civilization may constrain it. If our world is implemented under different host laws, our observations may describe only the child environment.

Which inferences are structural—for example, that some reliable implementation relation exists—and which merely remake the creator in our image?

Destination: Same-level technological reasoning and the Fermi Paradox do not automatically constrain an extra-universal host.

### Could we become creators?

Job: Study one concrete creator pathway without turning it into the definition of creation.

Could a sufficiently advanced civilization simulate conscious worlds, initiate baby universes, or create long-lived artificial habitats? Would a created world remain observable or controllable? How much survival time, energy, and technological capacity would the pathway require?

The Great Filter bears on creators descended from civilizations governed by physics like ours. It does not constrain every possible upstream implementation.

Research anchor:

- [Is it possible to create a universe in the laboratory by quantum tunneling?](https://doi.org/10.1016/055032139090357J) — Farhi, Guth, and Guven (1990)

### Does a parent count as a creator?

Job: Keep causal ancestry, initiation, implementation, design, and control separate.

A universe can cause another universe without designing it. A civilization can initiate a new region without controlling its contents. An implementation substrate need not resemble a digital computer.

Destination: `Creator` is not one relation. Every argument must say which additional property beyond causal priority it requires.

## Quantum and History-Space Research

### What can quantum mechanics actually contribute?

Job: Research mechanisms without using `quantum` as a synonym for mystery or purpose.

Questions promoted to the main outline include:

- why branch multiplicity does not license equal branch counting;
- whether a history space can be represented or searched efficiently;
- how path-integral weights differ from goal-directed optimization;
- what quantum search can accelerate;
- why free postselection is not an ordinary computational resource.

Research here should support those distinctions. It should not search for generic quantum features that merely look computational.

### Can a history space be useful without being searched?

Many-Worlds may contain a rich variety of trajectories while providing no agent with access to a cross-branch database. A generative physical process may still be valuable because it samples, instantiates, or weights emergent histories.

Destination: Separate representation, realization, sampling, conditioning, and searchable access.

## Speculative Correspondences

### Why do religions keep telling us how to behave?

This is a possible correspondence, not part of the derivation.

Which themes recur independently? How much do cultural evolution, psychology, cooperation, and group survival already explain? Would an Alignment Nursery independently predict moral development, judgment, restraint, compassion, or transcendence?

Destination: Interesting correspondence; presently weak and highly non-discriminating evidence.

### What happens when we die?

Artificial minds need not cease when one instantiation ends, but nothing in the project implies preservation.

The available possibilities include termination, archival, copying, reintegration, migration, and continuation in another substrate. Every afterlife-like conclusion requires additional assumptions about identity, creator policy, and available implementation.

Destination: Artificial implementation makes some forms of continuation structurally coherent. It does not establish that any continuation occurs.
